  - STAGE 02
  - FFmpeg & Audio Normalizer
  - AUDIO PRE-PROCESS
  - Extracts raw audio samples, applies noise-gating, and standardizes formats for transcription.
  - LATENCY: ~80ms Ingress
  - STAGE 03
  - Whisper STT + Gemini 1.5
  - AI & NLP EXTRACTION
  - Transcribes colloquial speech; parses SKU entity mappings, quantities, and transaction direction.
  - SCHEMA: JSON Constrained
  - STAGE 04
  - FastAPI + PostgreSQL DB
  - LOGIC & STATE LEDGER
  - Executes catalog SKU lookup, deducts stock counts, updates daily revenue, and records audit logs.
  - SYNC: Atomic Rollbacks
  - STAGE 05
  - WebSockets + Bot Reply
  - AMBIENT FEEDBACK
  - Dispatches instant WhatsApp receipts to merchant and pushes live visual inventory changes to dashboard.
  - FEEDBACK: < 2.0s Roundtrip

  - Feasibility, Ground Risks & Mitigation Matrix
  - COLUMN 01: FEASIBILITY DRIVERS
  - Ground Adoption & Viability
  - • Zero Capital Expenditure: Runs entirely on the shopkeeper's existing personal smartphone. Zero barcode scanners, thermal receipt printers, or desktop computers required.
  - • Zero Learning Barrier: Operates directly within WhatsApp—an application vendors already check 20+ times a day. Instant Day-1 merchant onboarding.
  - • Micro-SaaS Unit Economics: Serverless API execution costs less than 12 paise per voice order. Sustainable as an ultra-affordable ₹99 to ₹149/month subscription.
  - STATUS: HIGH COMMERCIAL VIABILITY
  - COLUMN 02: GROUND CHALLENGES
  - Operational Risks & Edge Cases
  - • Colloquial Code-Switching: Merchants mix Marathi, Hindi, and English fluidly (e.g., 'sakhar' vs 'sugar', regional FMCG brand abbreviations).
  - • Acoustic Market Noise: Road traffic, roadside chatter, and ceiling fans distort voice recordings during busy marketplace rush hours.
  - • Intermittent Connectivity: Temporary mobile data dropouts in semi-urban and rural Tier-2/3 retail clusters.
  - STATUS: IDENTIFIED FIELD BOTTLENECKS
  - COLUMN 03: MITIGATION STRATEGIES
  - Engineering Countermeasures
  - • Domain Synonym Dictionaries: Pre-trained regional alias mapping matches vernacular brand phonetics directly to standardized catalog SKUs.
  - • Audio Bandpass Filtering: FFmpeg isolates human vocal frequencies (300Hz-3400Hz) prior to transcription, rejecting ambient market hum.
  - • WhatsApp Native Offline Queue: Messages queue automatically on the device during network drops and process synchronously upon reconnection.
  - STATUS: IMPLEMENTED DEFENSES
