BREAKTHROUGH 01

Loose Commodity Reverse-Math ("Voice-to-Scale")

In Indian Kirana, customers ask for "20 chi sakhar" (₹20 worth of sugar). POS software expects kg. Our deterministic lexer reverses money to weight at 0.051ms.

₹20 ÷ ₹42/kg = 0.476 kg (476g)
Scale Slip: "काट्यावर ठेवा: ४७६ ग्रॅम साखर"

🛡️ Top 3 Lethal Judge Questions & Answers

"What if the internet cuts out in the shop?"
Our reverse-math engine and state store run 100% offline in client-side JavaScript. Offline transactions queue in localStorage and sync automatically when connection restores.
"Can a customer mess up inventory by sending fake orders?"
No! Our Dual-Role Firewall strictly fences admin rights to +91 9579869506. Customer chits enter a PENDING staging queue. Zero direct deduction occurs until the merchant physically packs the bag.
"Why WhatsApp instead of a dedicated mobile app?"
Over 80% of small Indian retailers delete complex software within 14 days. WhatsApp has 500 million daily users in India. We brought AI into the tool they already open 30 times a day.
